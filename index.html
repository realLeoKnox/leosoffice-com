02 / A LITTLE HISTORY

It began with
a website of my own.

Somehow I know quite a lot.
And somehow, nothing at all. 😭

— Leo

I’ve wanted a little place on the internet since primary school.

Back then, just having a website of my own seemed wonderful. In middle school, a QQ group introduced me to virtual hosting and “order-boosting” sites. That opened the door.

My first VPS came from a small one-person provider I found in another QQ group. They even charged me 30 to install a server control panel. I still laugh about that. 😂

A lot has changed since then. The curiosity that got me started hasn’t.

As for what I know now? Somehow it feels like quite a lot, and also like nothing at all. 😭

✳It starts with curiosity. Then, a world of possibilities.
Stay independent. Make things with care. Leave a little room for what comes next.✳
